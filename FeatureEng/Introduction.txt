Feature Scaling is a technique to standardize the independent features present in the dataset.
It is used to prevent features with large ranges from dominating the model.

Types for Feature Scaling
1. standardization
2. Normalization
    1. min max centre
    2. L1 Normalization

1. standardization
Also known as Z-score Normalization
It is used to scale the data to have a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1.
